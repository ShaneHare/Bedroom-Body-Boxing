Before you talk yourself out of it

The three things
everybody says first

These are the exact words Coach Lance hears at the door. Here's the honest answer to each.

"I haven't done boxing before."
Neither had anyone else in the room on their first day. Every class starts from stance and footwork, and you're shown what to do at each step. Nobody gets thrown in.
"I'm not in good shape to box."
Boxing is how you get in shape — not something you do after. You work at your own pace and rest when you need to. The conditioning arrives as a side effect.
"I don't have any coordination."
Coordination is the thing being taught. Nobody looks smooth in week one. The footwork gets broken down slow, and it clicks faster than you'd think.

Your coach

Coach Lance

Lance has been at this long enough to know that almost nobody walks through the door confident. They walk in thinking they're too old, too out of shape, too uncoordinated — and then they hit the bag for the first time and something shifts.

That's the reason he coaches. Not to build fighters, though some of them become that. To hand people the thing boxing handed him: proof that you can be bad at something on Monday and decent at it by Friday.

Every class is coached by Lance personally. No rotating staff, no screens, no watching a video and hoping you got it right.
