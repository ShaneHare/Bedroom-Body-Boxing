Before you talk yourself out of it

You start
where you are

These are the things people say before their first class. Here's the honest answer to each.

"I've never boxed before."
Most people here hadn't either. The class teaches fundamentals from the ground up — stance, footwork, combinations — and beginners learn at their own pace.
"I'm too out of shape."
You don't need to be in shape before you start. That's what the training is for. Exercises are modified to meet you where you are.
"Will I get hit?"
No. This is boxing conditioning — technique, footwork, bag and pad work. You are not going to be punched in a class.
"Do I have to spar?"
No. Sparring is not part of the conditioning class. Fighters who want that work arrange it separately with Coach Lance.
"Am I too old?"
No. Bedroom Body Boxing trains people well into their 60s and 70s, with the work adjusted to their ability and mobility.
"Can I keep up?"
You go at your pace and rest when you need to. The class runs at all levels at once — that's how it's built.
"Everyone else will be better than me."
Some will be. Others are on their first day. Nobody here is watching you — they're busy learning their own combinations.
"What if I have physical limitations?"
Tell Coach Lance before class. Training can be modified around ability, mobility and experience.
"Do I need my own equipment?"
Bring comfortable clothes, athletic shoes and water. Bring boxing gloves if you own them. Loaner equipment to be confirmed.

Meet your coach

20+ years.
Every level.

Bedroom Body Boxing started with a simple idea: you shouldn't have to be a fighter to train like one.

Built on more than 20 years of boxing and coaching experience, Coach Lance developed an approach that takes the discipline, movement, conditioning, technique and confidence of boxing and makes them accessible to everyday people.

That philosophy has brought an unusually diverse group together. Healthcare professionals and business professionals train alongside athletes. Complete beginners learn the fundamentals while experienced boxers sharpen their skills. Children and teenagers build coordination, discipline and confidence. Local grandmothers and grandfathers discover that boxing conditioning can meet them at their level too.

At the competitive end, Lance's experience lets him work with athletes ranging from amateur competitors to professional fighters.

Different ages. Different professions. Different goals. Different starting points. One community.

Bedroom Body Boxing isn't about proving how tough you are. It's about discovering how strong you can become.

First-timer FAQ

Before you come in

Starting out
I've never boxed. Can I still join?

Yes — that's who the class is built for. You'll learn the fundamentals from your first session, at your own pace.

Do I have to be in shape before starting?

No. You don't need to get fit before you join; the training is how you get fit. Exercises are modified to your level.

Are classes really appropriate for beginners?

Yes. No previous boxing experience is required, and beginners train in the same class as everyone else with the work scaled to them.

Can I try a class before joining?

Yes. Your first class is free — come and see whether it's for you before committing to anything.

What actually happens
Will I get hit?

No. This is boxing conditioning — technique, footwork, bag and pad work. You will not be punched in a class.

Do I have to spar?

No. Sparring isn't part of the conditioning class.

How long is each class?

One hour.

Can exercises be modified?

Yes. Training is adjusted around ability, mobility, conditioning and experience. Tell Coach Lance anything he should know before you start.

What to bring
What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable workout clothing, athletic shoes and water. Bring boxing gloves if you already own them.

Do I need my own boxing gloves?

Bring them if you have them. Whether loaner gloves are available is still to be confirmed.

How early should I arrive?

Ten to fifteen minutes before your first class, so there's time to complete your waiver and get set up.

Where should I park?

Parking details to be confirmed.

Who trains here
Can experienced boxers train here?

Yes. Experienced participants keep developing technique and conditioning in the same class, and Lance works with amateur and professional fighters.

Do you train children and teenagers?

Yes — with training built around a young athlete's pace, focused on fundamentals, coordination, discipline and confidence.

Do you train older adults?

Yes, with the work modified appropriately based on individual ability, mobility, conditioning and experience.

Do you offer women's training?

Yes. Women's sessions are part of the program.

Do you train athletes from other sports?

Yes. Boxing conditioning builds footwork, coordination, hand speed and an engine that carries into other sports.

Do you offer corporate or group sessions?

Yes — corporate and small-group training are available. Get in touch about booking a group.
